## Sidequest System Core
###


---

### Quick Character Creation Guide
1. Select your **Training** and take it's **Training Specialty**
2. Decide your first **Path**
3. Pick a **Trope** (or create your own)
4. Select a **Strength** (or create your own)
5. Pick a **Flaw** (or create your own)
6. Assign your **Stat Dice**
7. Answer the **Background Questions**

---

### Training Options

#### Artifact Handler
De-possesses delicate or high-value items (instruments, display pieces) without altering function or finish
#### Cleanup Specialist
Neutralizes glamour residue, collects trace, and certifies a space safe for normal use
#### Containment Tech
Pins object-bound entities using coil packs, salt foggers, and grounded iron spikes; handles the "box it and move it" step
#### Crowd Liason
Manages clients and bystanders on-site, frames expectations, and buys the crew five quiet minutes when it matters
#### Field Tinkerer
Keeps coil packs, couplers, and projectors alive under stress
#### Negotiator
Handles etiquette and offerings; convinces a rider to relocate or stand down without damage to the object
#### Research Archivist
Fast-pulls object histories, serial runs, and Court tells; updates case cards and the app's knowledge base
#### Runner/Driver
Shields and transports volatile items in a surge-protected van; routes around interference hot spots
#### Sensor Operator
Sweeps with EM/sigil scanners, thermal Polaroids, and ring-light glyph projectors to find which object is ridden
#### Ward Carpenter
Installs copper/iron hardware, chalk lines, and retrofit plates so sites don't repossess after the team leaves.

---

### Path Options

Once you have selected your first **Training** you decide whether to start down the Wyrd Path, giving you access to **Spell Tags**, or the Mundane Path, giving you access to tools and skills that help you do your job.
Whichever path you choose, you get Keystone training for your **Training**.

---

### Tropes, Strengths, Flaws

The **Tropes**, **Strengths**, and **Flaws** in this document are just suggestions, if none of them seem to fit your character you can work with your GM to come up with something that will work. To help with balance your ability should work at most once per scene, or once per job if it is extra powerful.

---

### Stats

When creating a character you assign each of your dice (**d4/d6/d8/10/d12/20**) to one of the stats, based on what you want your character to be good at. Each Training has a recommended main and secondary stat (where to put your **d20/d12**) but these are only recommendations, your character is yours and you do what feels best to you.

#### Brains
Used for research, analysis, tech, and planning

#### Brawn
Used for lifting, breaking, carrying, and anything requiring the use of force

#### Charm
Used for social leverage, deception, and command

#### Fight
Used for combat, grapples, and possession

#### Flight
Used for speed, stealth, driving, and escapes

#### Grit
Used for fear control, endurance, holding the line, and resisting **Environmental Wyrd**

#### Mundane Path Gear Options
First contact with a ridden object doesn’t auto-trigger it.

**Finish-Safe Extraction (1)**
Remove an entity/object without damaging function/appearance.
If it would cause **Exposure**, you may take **Marked** instead.
**Limit:** 1/scene.

**Trigger Diagnosis (1)**
Learn what sets an object off and what calms it down.
(If contested/obscure, roll Brains vs TN; failure still gives one true fact and Marked—GM call.)

**Secure Storage Protocols (2)**
Once/job, “store” a problem safely until downtime. Stable, not solved.
If storage is sloppy or rushed: **Exposure +1d4** (GM call).

**Provenance Knife (3)**
Cut an ownership chain (symbolically). Remove one layer of claim/protection.
If contested, roll Charm or Brains vs TN 12.

#### Spell Tags

---

**Banish**

* **Small:** Cleanse **one object** of **possession**; the presence is forced out and cannot cling to that object for a **beat**.
* **Medium:** Cleanse **up to 3 objects**, *or* cleanse **one large object** (e.g., a couch, a door, a vehicle); the presence is forced out and cannot re-enter those objects for a **segment**.
* **Large:** Cleanse **all possessed objects within one defined room/zone**; presences are forced out and cannot re-enter objects in that space for the **scene**.

**Sense**

* **Small:** Get **one clear read** (one concrete answer) about **one thing** in the immediate situation.
* **Medium:** Get **two reads**, *or* one read plus **directional context** (near/far, above/below, toward/away).
* **Large:** Get **three reads**, and you may ask **the primary hazard or trigger** active in the room.

**Step**

* **Small:** Move to a new position **within the same room/zone** using a seam route (shadow edge, reflection, door crack).
* **Medium:** Move into an **adjacent space** (through one barrier/threshold), or bring **one willing ally** with you.
* **Large:** Move across **multiple connected spaces** in the scene (room→hall→stairwell, etc.), or bring **up to 3 allies** through the same seam-route.

**Unravel**

* **Small:** Unmake **one small possessed object** into harmless basic parts (e.g., a doll into stuffing, a book into loose pages); it cannot function until repaired.
* **Medium:** Unmake **one object** (or **up to 3 small objects**) into basic parts (e.g., a blanket into yarn, a chair into boards and nails); it cannot function until repaired.
* **Large:** Unmake **one large object** (or a tightly connected set of objects) into basic parts (e.g., a couch into fabric and frame, a door into planks and hardware); it cannot function until repaired.

**Wardmark**

* **Small:** Mark **one object**; while marked, it **cannot be possessed** (possessions fails to take hold).
* **Medium:** Mark **up to 3 objects**, *or* **one larger object** (e.g., a couch, a door, a vehicle component); while marked, they **cannot be possessed**.
* **Large:** Mark **all objects within one defined room/area**; while marked, objects there **cannot be possessed**.

#### Wyrd Upgrades

**Signature Spells**

---

Pick **one** of your spell tags as your **Signature**. Choose **one Tune** for it:

* **Quiet:** no obvious visuals unless you want them
* **Fast:** once per scene, cast your Signature as a free “minor action”
* **Stable:** on your Signature, “any die shows a 1” doesn’t add Wyrd (still marks Strain) **1/scene**
* **Anchored:** your Signature lasts one step longer (beat→segment→scene)
* **Split:** your Signature can affect **+1 target** at the same scale
* **Coded:** your Signature looks mundane on sensors unless someone is actively scanning for it

---

**Echo Recording**

---

Your magic “records” reality and can play it back.

* Each time you cast, you may capture one **Echo**: a 1–2 second sensory snippet (sound, reflection, heat-image, smell, a sentence).
* Later in the job, you can release an Echo to:

    * **prove** something happened (to an NPC/system),
    * **bait** something (trigger, lure, distraction),
    * **reconstruct** a clue (“show me what the hallway looked like before it looped”).
* **Cost:** Echos are **sticky**. Holding more than 1 at a time makes you feel “observed” (GM can justify Marked/pressure when it fits).

#### Spell Tags

**Bindname**

* **Small:** Choose **one target**; it must present as what it truly is (disguise/false shape/false category can’t hold) for a **beat**.
* **Medium:** Choose **one target**; the truth-lock holds for a **segment**, *or* affects **up to 3 targets** for a **beat**.
* **Large:** Choose **one source phenomenon** in an **entire room** (one spirit, one illusion engine, one curse web); everything it projects in that room must present honestly for the **scene**.

* **Small:** Reduce **Wyrd by 1** in one small focus (one person/object/one 5-ft spot); the air “clears” and the pressure drops.

* **Medium:** Draw a boundary that encloses a **small area** (a circle around a workstation, a ritual pocket, a corner of a room). No extraplanar creature can cross this threshold.
* **Large:** Draw a boundary that encloses **an entire room** (or seals multiple exits of that room as one working). No extraplanar creature can cross this threshold.

* **Small:** Get **one clear read** (one concrete answer) about **one thing** in the immediate situation.

**Residue Economy**

Magic leaves **usable residue**, and you know how to work with it.

* When you cast, you may choose to leave a **Residue Tag** in the scene (static smear, salt bloom, mirrored scab, burnt sigil, fog of incense, etc.).
* Once per scene, anyone can leverage a Residue Tag for a **situational advantage** (track the hazard, lock a route, reveal a hidden seam, justify a prep, etc.).
* **Cost:** each Residue Tag is also a **trail**. The GM can use it later to justify attention (authorities, entities, rivals, “it shows up in the records”).

#### Mundane Path Options

**Salt Fogger (—)**
Create a **Containment Zone** (one room or clear 20–30 ft area).

* **Zone rule:** the **first failure** made by anyone inside the Zone **does not mark a setback**.
* That roll still: **gains Momentum**.
  **Limit:** 1/scene.

---

**Coil Pack “Buster” (1)**
When you add **+1 or +2 Progress** on a containment action, you may **Latch** the phenomenon.

* Next time it tries to shift/escape/phase, it must pay one (GM picks):

    * **Collateral:** break something loud/visible → **Exposure +1** if witnesses/systems can notice,
    * **Swap:** trade places with an object/feature,
    * **Lose Ground:** your next roll against it this scene gets **+1**.
      **Limit:** 1/scene.

---

**Iron Spike Kit (1)**
Lock a **Threshold** (door/window/stairs/marked line).

* The phenomenon can’t cross unless it does one:

    * **Bleed Pressure:** **reduce Wyrd by 1d4** (min 0) and the lock breaks, or
    * **Break Through:** smash it physically (GM sets TN/obstacle).
      **Limit:** 2 thresholds/scene.

---

**The Box (2)**
When a containment scene would mark its **final setback**, you can instead **end the scene with the target contained**.
Choose the price (pick 1):

* **Exposure +2**,
* **Everyone involved becomes Marked**,
* **Unstable Containment:** the Box becomes a downtime problem that must be handled before the next job,
* **Collateral:** something important breaks / someone gets hurt / the site becomes hostile.
  **Limit:** 1/job.

---

**Lockdown Surge (3)**
When **Wyrd hits 6**, delay the **Reality Surge** until the **end of the current beat**.
**Limit:** 1/job.

**Rule-Locking**

Your spells can temporarily **set a local rule** the world follows.

* When you cast, you may add one **Rule Clause** to the effect (pick one):

    * “No crossing,” “No lies,” “No names spoken,” “No violence,” “No cameras,” “No reflections,” “No open flames,” etc.
* The clause applies only within the spell’s affected space and duration.
* **Cost:** Rule Clauses always have a **loophole**. The GM states it when it matters (not immediately), and entities will find it.

### Tropes, Strengths, Flaws

#### Tropes

* **By-the-Book:** Once per job, you can produce the **right form/permit/phrase** to reduce **Exposure clock** by **1** (or cancel one complication).
* **Conspiracy Nut:** Once per scene, when you make a wild theory, roll **Brains**; on a success you get **one true clue** (even if your theory is wrong).
* **Cynical Field Agent:** Once per scene, when you call out the obvious downside, you may **ask for the real cost** of a plan; the GM must name one concrete consequence to watch for.
* **Down-on-their-Luck:** When you fail an investigation/social read, gain **+2 Momentum** instead of +1.
* **Ex-Specialist:** Once per job, you can **downgrade a TN by 1 step** for a roll you’ve “done a hundred times”
* **Glitchy Gadgeteer:** Once per job, you can **jury-rig** a device to create a one-off effect.
* **Jaded Survivor:** Once per scene, when Wyrd increases, you may **ignore becoming Shaken** from that moment.
* **“Just Doing My Job”:** Once per scene, ignore **one Condition penalty** (−1) on a roll if you describe the calm, procedural routine you fall into.
* **The “Normal” One (no one believes you):** Once per job, you can make a mundane explanation stick; **erase 1 point of Exposure** (witnesses reinterpret what they saw).
* **Occult Freelancer:** You start each job with **one extra contact** “on retainer” (one call/text for a useful fact, tool, or introduction).
* **Reluctant Hero, Reluctant Believer:** The first time per scene you step in to protect someone, gain **+1 Momentum** immediately.
* **True Believer in the Thin Places:** Once per job, when you cast in a thin place, you may **treat Wyrd as 1 lower** for determining escalation/TN bump (doesn’t reduce the track—just your threshold).
* **Veteran:** Once per job, declare a **classified detail** you “already know” about a site/entity; take **+1** on the next roll that uses it.
* **Wannabe Influencer of the Supernatural:** Once per job, you can **manufacture a distraction** that buys a beat: take **+1** on the next Charm/Flight roll to redirect a crowd or attention.
* **“Wyrdness Magnet”:** Once per scene, you can ask the GM: **“What’s the Wyrdest thing here?”** and get a straight, actionable answer.

### Playing the Game

#### Rolls

**Roll:** roll your **stat die**, apply any **Condition penalty**, compare to TN (or opposed roll).

**Condition penalty:** if a relevant Condition applies, take **−1** to the roll.

---
#### Blowup

On a **max result**, add that max and roll **one die size higher**, then add the new roll.

**Chain:** you may blow up again if you roll max again.

**d20:** on a d20 max, add **20** and reroll **d20**.

**Magic blowup:** if the die that blows up is your **Magic Die**, **Wyrd +1**.

---
#### Momentum (earned on failure)

Momentum is personal, not shared.



**Gain:** When you **fail a roll**, gain **+1 Momentum**.
When you **fail a roll** by **5 or more** gain **+2 Momentum**.



You may have a maximum of **10 Momentum** at one time. If you have **10 Momentum** you cannot earn more until you have spent some.

Momentum **carries over between jobs**. It is not reset at the end of a job, which is what makes the more expensive Group Bonuses reachable.

---

**Spend Momentum (choose one):**

* **Push (1):** Add a +1 to a roll

* **Flashback (2):** declare a reasonable prep you already did (packed gear, called a contact, pulled a map)

* **Stunt (3):** cinematic repositioning or environmental interaction that changes the situation

* **Teamwork Makes the Dream Work (3):** once per job a player may give half the value of one of their stat die to an ally

---
#### Handoff

**Rule:** On your turn, you may **transfer 1 Momentum** to an ally you can see/hear (or via comms).
**Cost 2:1**

> Lets teams coordinate without needing a failure right now, but still has a price.

#### Possession

When a phenomenon tries to take you (or force your body to act), roll **Fight or Grit** vs the hazard TN.

* **Success:** you keep control.
* **Failure:** you lose control for **one beat** *or* you’re forced into a single action (GM call). Also apply the normal failure effects (mark failure, Wyrd +1, gain +1 Momentum).

---
#### Wyrd

Every contested scene (chase, containment, fight, tense negotiation under pressure) has a **Wyrd Track: 0 → 6**.

#### When Wyrd increases

Use whichever combination fits the scene:

1. **Failure-driven Wyrd (default):** Each failed roll adds **+1 Wyrd**.

2. **Time pressure:** In unstable locations, Wyrd increases **+1 per scene beat**

    * A “beat” is typically every 2–3 rounds in combat, or each major location shift in a chase.

3. **Magic stress:** On any casting roll where **any die shows a 1**, add **+1 Wyrd** and mark **Strain** (see Conditions).

This ensures escalation even when the team is competent, and it makes failure meaningfully change the texture of reality.

#### Wyrd reduction

**Wyrd drops only when something stabilizes it** (a stabilizing scene, CLEANSE, or a group asset). If Wyrd drops below **6**, the “Wyrd 6” escalation stops.

---
#### Wyrd threshold

* **Wyrd 2 — Environmental Distortion:** lights, sounds, looping, “wrong” acoustics, unreliable directionality.

* **Wyrd 4 — Hazard Active:** boundary snap, cold pockets, shadow latch, bleed to other realms begins.

* **Wyrd 6 — The Scene Gets Wyrd:** reality-altering surge. Objects become actively possessed, two spaces overlap, the environment re-skins (indoor forest, underwater building, suddenly high in a tree), and the scene becomes a set piece.


#### Exposure

**Exposure** = how close the Wyrd is to going public.

**Mark Exposure (failure):** witnesses/cameras catch it, alarms/damage, obvious magic, cops/security escalate, evidence left behind.

**Clear Exposure (success):** evacuate/distract, cover story/permits, block sightlines/relocate, secure footage, clean residue/patch damage.

**At 3 failures:** the scene continues, but a consequence hits (authorities arrive, footage spreads, site gets restricted, new complication clock).

#### Wyrd 6 Reality Surge

When Wyrd hits 6:

* The GM triggers **one Reality Surge** immediately.

* Then, at the top of each round/beat, the GM triggers a **minor escalation** until the scene ends or Wyrd is reduced.

* Wyrd can only be reduced narratively.

**Reality Surge examples (pick one):**

* **Possessed Set Dressing:** doors slam and lock, furniture attacks, elevators become predatory.

* **Space Overlay:** the room overlaps with a second biome (forest/reef/high canopy/ruins); movement rules change.

\page
* **Perception Inversion:** up/down swap, echoes speak first, shadows arrive before bodies.

* **Boundary Merge:** two locations become one; a stairwell exits into an impossible place.

* **Flood/Fire/Cold Skin:** the building behaves as though underwater / on fire / inside a freezer.

---

#### Stabilizing Scene

A stabilizing scene is **1–3 minutes** of uncontested control (perimeter set, wards up, breathing room, no active pursuit).

At the end of it, the crew chooses **one**:

* **Clear 1 Condition** from one PC
* **Reduce Wyrd by 1** in the current zone

---

#### Encounters = Clocks

Every “battle” can be combat, chase, containment, negotiation under pressure, or rescue. The structure stays the same:

Create a **Clock**: “X successes before Y failures.”
Examples:

* **Containment 6 before 3**
* **Chase 5 before 3**
* **Extraction 6 before 2**
* **Exposure 4 before 3** (avoid public visibility)

Each meaningful action roll:

* success advances the clock (+1; +2 on strong success)
* failure marks a failure, increases Wyrd (+1), and earns the player Momentum (+1)

This produces your desired rhythm: frequent contested sequences with escalating Wyrd.

---

#### Conditions

Conditions apply −1 ongoing to relevant rolls until cleared.

* **Shaken** (fear/unease)- **Fight/Grit**
* **Winded** (fatigue)- **Brawn/Fight**
* **Distracted** (sensory distortion)- **Brains/Charm**
* **Marked** (the phenomenon “knows you”)- **Flight/Charm**
* **Strained** (magic overload)- **Grit/Brains**

Clear conditions with rest, support, ritual care, medical help, safehouse downtime, or a successful stabilizing scene.

### Using Wyrd

#### Casting with Scale

1. **Pick spell tag** (Wardmark, Glamour, etc.)
2. **Pick scale** (S/M/L).
3. **Roll**: Casting Stat **Magic Die** (+Skill if relevant)
4. **TN by scale**:

* **Small:** TN **9** (Standard)
* **Medium:** TN **12** (Hard)
* **Large:** TN **15** (Severe)

**Escalation rule:** If the target is actively resisting, the area is unstable, or **Wyrd ≥4**, bump TN **up one step** (S→14, M→17, L→20+).

**The stress rule stays:** if **any die shows a 1**, **Wyrd +1**.

---

#### What “Small / Medium / Large” means in play

Use these as *default magnitudes*; each spell below translates them into specifics.

* **Small:** single object/target, or a **5-ft square/one threshold**.
* **Medium:** a small room/zone feature, or **up to ~3 targets**, lasts **a scene segment**.
* **Large:** an **entire room / major scene element**, or **up to ~6 targets**, lasts **the scene** (until broken/overruled).

The die assigned to a stat (d4/d6/d8/d10/d12/d20). Roll it for actions using that stat.

**Step (Spell Tag)**
A spell tag for seam-route movement. See **STEP** under Spell Tags.

**Strained (Condition)**
Magic overload. −1 when casting, focusing, or resisting magical stress.

**Stress Rule (Casting)**
On any casting roll where any die shows a 1: Wyrd +1 and mark Strained.

**Stunt (Momentum Spend)**
Spend 3 Momentum for a cinematic reposition/environment interaction that changes the situation.

**Strong Success**
A higher-quality success that advances the clock by +2 (as written).

**Team Protocol (Group Upgrade)**
A once-per-job group maneuver unlocked by spending group Momentum.

**Thermal Polaroids**
Sensor module: ask one SENSE question with gear; answer arrives as an image artifact.

**Thin Place**
A location where reality is weak, overlaps, or “rules” are easier to break.

**Thin-Place Etiquette**
Tier 2 Interface option: once per scene ask what the place demands; pay it to gain a reality privilege.

**Threshold**
A boundary line in space (doorway, window, stairwell mouth, etc.) that can be reinforced or ruled.

**TN (Target Number)**
The number you must meet or beat on a roll to succeed (or oppose another roll).

**Training**
Your job specialty track (Containment Tech, Sensor Operator, etc.) with two paths: Wyrd Path and Mundane Path.

**Tune (Tier 1)**
A modifier chosen for your Signature spell tag: Quiet/Fast/Stable/Anchored/Split/Coded.

**Unravel (Spell Tag)**
A spell tag that un-makes possessed objects into harmless parts. See **UNRAVEL** under Spell Tags.

**Vehicle Mastery**
Strength: first success in chase/drive/pilot clocks counts as +2.

**Ward Carpenter**
Training focused on wards, thresholds, safe zones, and rule-based barriers.

**Wardmark (Spell Tag)**
A spell tag that prevents possession of marked objects/areas. See **WARDMARK** under Spell Tags.

**Winded (Condition)**
Fatigue. −1 when exertion, speed, endurance, or sustained effort matters.

**Wyrd**
The reality-pressure of a contested scene, tracked from 0 to 6.

**Wyrd 2 (Environmental Distortion)**
Lights/sounds loop, “wrong” acoustics, unreliable directionality.

**Wyrd 4 (Hazard Active)**
Boundary snap, cold pockets, shadow latch, bleed begins.

**Wyrd 6 (The Scene Gets Wyrd)**
A reality-altering surge; environment re-skins; objects become actively possessed; space overlays/merges.

**Wyrd Path**
The magic side of a Training track (Wyrd-1 through Wyrd-6).

**Wyrd Reduction**
Wyrd only drops when something stabilizes it (Stabilizing Scene, CLEANSE, or a group asset).

**Wyrd Tier (0–3)**
A progression track for Wyrd Upgrades: Tier 1 Tune, Tier 2 Interface, Tier 3 Spellcraft.

**Wyrd Upgrade**
A replacement for a gear pick: instead of taking gear, raise Wyrd Tier by 1 and gain that tier’s benefit.

**Wyrd-1 / Wyrd-2 / …**
The named nodes on the Wyrd Path (die increases and key magic options).
